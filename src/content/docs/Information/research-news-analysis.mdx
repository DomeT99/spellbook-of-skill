## Description

This guide teaches the skill of researching current topics effectively — filtering noise, identifying key stories, and summarizing findings for decision-making.

## Guide

Find the [number] most important recent stories in the field of [topic] and summarize each with context and key takeaways.

## Example

"Find the five most important recent stories in software development."

- **GitHub supply-chain attack using invisible Unicode characters**: The "Glassworm" campaign compromised repositories by hiding malicious code.
- **Java SE 26 released**: New features include API removals and platform improvements.
- **Open-source dependency vulnerabilities grow**: Most organizations run software with exploitable third-party dependencies.
- **Microsoft expands quantum development tools**: New open-source tools for quantum software.
- **Emerging systems languages gain momentum**: Memory-safe alternatives to C and C++.

## Explanation

### Key Skills

1. **Source Evaluation**: Identify credible and current sources of information.
2. **Filtering**: Focus on the most relevant stories for your specific field.
3. **Synthesis**: Distill complex news into clear, actionable summaries.
4. **Contextualization**: Explain why each story matters and its implications.

### Best Use Cases

- ✅ Professionals staying updated in their industry
- ✅ Researchers tracking developments
- ✅ Decision-makers needing quick briefings
- ✅ Content curators monitoring trends

### Tips for Best Results

- Be specific about your field of interest
- Focus on trends, not isolated events
- Combine with date ranges for more precise results
